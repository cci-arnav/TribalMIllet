Our story

Food that nourished generations, before it nourished us

TribalMillet began with a simple belief: the food that nourishes us best is the food that has nourished generations before us.

Where it started

A grain built for hard land

For centuries, tribal farming communities across India have grown millets — hardy, nutrient-rich grains suited to dry, marginal land where few other crops survive. Yet as diets shifted toward polished rice and wheat, millet farming declined, and with it, a source of livelihood for many tribal families.

TribalMillet was founded to reverse that story.

How we work

A short, honest supply chain

We work directly with tribal farmer collectives, paying fair prices for their harvest and helping restore traditional millet farming as a sustainable, dignified livelihood.

Every product we sell — from whole grains to ready-to-cook mixes — is sourced this way: transparently, respectfully, and with the farmer's wellbeing built into the price. This is more than a food brand; it's a small, honest supply chain connecting your kitchen to the people who grow what you eat.

Tribal Farmer Registration

If you're a tribal farmer or part of a farming collective growing millets, register with us. Our procurement team reviews every application and reaches out directly to discuss sourcing, fair pricing, and onboarding.

Fair, transparent pricing

We pay above local mandi rates and settle payments directly to farmers, with no middlemen.

Simple onboarding

Our field team verifies your details and visits your village to complete registration and quality checks.
